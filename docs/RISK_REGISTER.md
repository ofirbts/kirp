**Likelihood:** Low / Medium / High (subjective but grounded in exposure—e.g. internet-facing route without auth = High exploitability surface).

**Owner:** `UNASSIGNED` until your org assigns DRI.

## Severity definitions (operational)

- **P0:** Cross-tenant confidentiality or integrity break, or unauthenticated remote mutation plausible on default router exposure.
- **P1:** Integrity/availability under misconfig or false confidence; broad read exfiltration without guessable IDs.
- **P2:** Degraded correctness, ops pain, or bounded edge cases.
- **P3:** Polish, doc-only, or low-likelihood niche.

## Production blocking status

**Hard block multi-tenant production** until R-001, R-002 (for exposed paths), R-003, R-005, R-006, R-007, R-012 are closed or mitigated with **documented compensating controls** (e.g. private network + separate auth gateway—not “trust the perimeter” without evidence).
